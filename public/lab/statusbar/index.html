Status-bar lab

Why

The design prefers a fully light top in light mode (L1). Whether an installed iPhone app can show a dark clock in light mode and a light clock in dark mode cannot be confirmed from documentation. These four pages answer it on your phone.

How to test — about five minutes

  1. In Safari on the iPhone, open A below.
  2. Share → Add to Home Screen → Add. It appears as “Lab A”.
  3. Do the same for B, C and D (each from its own page, so each keeps its settings).
  4. Open each one from the Home Screen (not Safari). The page says “Installed app: Yes”.
  5. Follow the four checks on the page, in Light and in Dark. A screenshot of the top of each, in each mode, is the clearest answer — 8 screenshots.
  6. Tell me for each: Light — clock dark and readable? Dark — clock light and readable? Any coloured strip that does not match the page?
  7. Then delete the four Lab icons from the Home Screen.

The variants

A
L1 · default style, theme-color per mode
B
L1 · default style, no theme-color
C
L2 · black-translucent, Midnight cap (today's behaviour)
D
L1 · one theme-color switched live

What decides it

If A (or D) is readable in both modes, the app moves to L1. If neither is, it keeps L2 with the smallest possible Midnight cap (C). Nothing here reads, stores or sends any data.
